Mario Hossen is an Austro-Bulgarian soloist of international acclaim. He has performed with more than 100 orchestras worldwide, including the London Philharmonic Orchestra, English Chamber Orchestra, Academy of St Martin in the Fields, Vienna Symphony Orchestra, Bruckner Orchestra Linz, Orchestra Wiener Akademie, Vienna Chamber Orchestra, Wiener Concert-Verein, Sinfonia Varsovia, Bulgarian National Radio Symphony Orchestra, Sofia Philharmonic Orchestra, I Solisti Veneti, Tchaikovsky Symphony Orchestra of Moscow Radio, RTV Slovenia Symphony Orchestra, I Virtuosi del Teatro alla Scala, Chamber Orchestra of the Bavarian Radio Symphony Orchestra, Franz Liszt Chamber Orchestra, Helsinki Baroque Orchestra, Orquesta Sinfónica del Estado de México, Presidential Symphony Orchestra of Turkey, Istanbul State Symphony Orchestra, and Polish Baltic Philharmonic Fryderyk Chopin.

Renowned for his extraordinary virtuosity and magnetic stage presence, his repertoire spans from Renaissance and Classical traditions to contemporary music and Jazz/Tango.

Hossen was born in Plovdiv, Bulgaria. His mother, Nelly Batchvarova, was an opera singer, while his father was a lawyer. His heritage reflects a blend of four nationalities. He has been based in Vienna since 1991, and in 1995 he was granted Austrian citizenship in recognition of his special contributions to the Republic.  He received his musical education in Sofia, Vienna, and Paris. He began playing the piano at the age of five and started his violin studies a year later in Plovdiv with his first teacher, Atanas Batchvarov. His subsequent mentors include Darina Dankova, Michael Frischenschlager, and Gérard Poulet.  He made his debut as a soloist with orchestra at the age of eight. Since then, he has performed in concert halls across more than 40 countries worldwide.

Mario Hossen is the recipient of numerous prestigious awards, including the City of Sofia’s “Musician of the Year” distinction. Under his artistic direction, the Varna Summer International Music Festival – the oldest music festival in Bulgaria – was recognized by the European Festivals Association as an EFFE Laureate for the 2019–2020 season and received the European Audience Award.  Internationally acclaimed as a leading violin pedagogue, he serves as Professor at New Bulgarian University and was formerly a faculty member of the Escuela Superior de Música Reina Sofía.  His students have earned top prizes at major international competitions, including the David Oistrakh Competition, Tibor Varga Competition, Kocian Violin Competition, and Maria Canals International Music Competition. Many have gone on to successful careers as concertmasters, orchestral musicians, and soloists across Europe and Asia.

He approaches each work with exceptional depth, placing strong emphasis on historical context and the highest standards of stylistic authenticity. His musicological and artistic work is particularly devoted to source research on Niccolò Paganini, whose oeuvre occupies a central place in his career. He is widely regarded as one of the leading interpreters and authorities on Paganini’s music.  The complete works of Paganini for solo violin and for violin and orchestra have been published as a historical-critical edition under his direction by Doblinger in the “Diletto Musicale” series (Vienna).

Mario Hossen has released 26 CD albums for the Italian labels Dynamic and Da Vinci Classics, as well as Hänssler Classic and Doron Music. His extensive discography includes the complete violin concertos of Niccolò Paganini, Max Bruch, Felix Mendelssohn, and Johannes Brahms, as well as the complete 15 string quartets with guitar by Paganini. It further features the complete violin sonatas of Ludwig van Beethoven, alongside works by Johann Sebastian Bach and George Frideric Handel.  His recordings also include numerous world premiere releases of previously unpublished works by Niccolò Paganini, Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, Giuseppe Tartini, Alessandro Rolla, and Saverio Mercadante.  Among the composers who have dedicated works to him are Rainer Bischof, Gheorghi Arnaoudov, Tomás Marco, François-Pierre Descamps, Periklis Liakakis, Roumen Dimitrov, Alessandro Solbiati, and Walter Baer. 

As a conducting soloist, he leads the Vienna Chamber Orchestra and Les Orphesites Orchestra in the concert series “Concert Spirituel” in Vienna.

Mario Hossen has also been invited to be a Jury member at important international violin competitions, including the “Jascha Heifetz” Violin Competition (Vilnius), the “Fritz Kreisler” Violin Competition (Vienna), the “Ginette Neveu” Violin Competition (Avignon), the “Tibor Varga” Violin Competition (Sion), “Alfred Binner” Violin Competition, and other competitions in Tokyo, Sofia and Vienna.

He has been an artist of Thomastik-Infeld since 2008 and performs exclusively on his preferred strings by the company.  

In 2019, Mario Hossen initiated a project to record the complete string quartets and chamber music works of Niccolò Paganini. In this context, he founded the Paganini Ensemble Vienna, an ensemble dedicated to Paganini’s chamber music. In 2024, the Ensemble was appointed Ambassador of the European Paganini Route, an EU cultural initiative of the City of Genoa. He is also an Honorary Member of the „Amici di Paganini“ in Genoa, in recognition of his outstanding contributions to preserving and promoting Paganini’s musical legacy. 

Mario Hossen performs on a violin by Giovanni Battista Guadagnini (1749), kindly loaned to him by the Austrian National Bank.
